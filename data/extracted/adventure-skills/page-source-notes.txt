Adventure creation Skills page: fixed and generated native source domains

page-skills-sources.tsv is a native source/data inventory assembled from
page-literals.tsv, the retained page disassembly, and the existing authored
local-overrides.tsv / translations.tsv dictionaries. It is not a runtime
result, simulated display, test case, regression fixture, or game observation.

The retained instruction range is reference RVA 0x628aaf..0x62bbfc and classic
RVA 0x6264cf..0x62961c, with the common creation header and six tabs added from
their own instructions. The TSV keeps each edition's native instruction and
literal RVA rather than deduplicating away provenance. It contains 310
instruction/domain records, including both closing-quote operands and six
generated header formatter-domain records. The PE image identities are
PE:6a70a6d9:02711000 and PE:6a70b91c:0270a000.

The selected range has 45 distinct complete hover sentences. These and the
fixed action/headings already have authored complete Chinese translations.
The source operands preserve native spelling errors such as "stamina recover"
and "minor affect"; their exact authored rows translate the intended meaning.
No complete fixed sentence in this native slice needs a new dictionary phrase.

Source roles and translation ownership:

* Fixed page captions and actions are complete strings: Accept attributes and
  skills, Archetypes, Customize, Your Attributes and Skills. Name and the
  Background / Skills / Appearance / Personality / Equipment / Mount and pets
  captions belong to the common creation header. The TSV retains their exact
  authored dictionary row and flags.

* Attributes remaining: and Skill remaining: are native prefixes followed by
  the separately formatted integer. They use existing complete templates
  Attributes remaining: {d} and Skill remaining: {d}, not independently
  translated prefix fragments. Their final values depend on the character.

* Attribute rating and attribute name operands are parts of one native string,
  assembled before drawing. The native seven-by-nineteen source productions
  are in attribute-productions.tsv. Existing full case-insensitive wil phrases
  cover both lowercase comparison-card and Title Case customization variants.
  Missing standalone words such as "Above Average" do not imply a missing
  complete translation; adding fragment rules would lose the native field's
  semantics. The comparison formatter skips Average and the custom formatter
  includes it, as documented in attribute-formatters.txt.

* Numeric cost suffix operands " pt" and " pts" belong to the complete
  existing {d} pt and {d} pts templates. One point takes the singular suffix;
  other quantities take plural. Both forms are authored in the current
  dictionary. Native integer operands are values,
  not new English prose. Attribute costs and clipping are documented in
  attribute-formatters.txt; skill costs are drawn independently of names too.

* Not is the native zero-skill rating operand at reference 0x629afe and classic
  0x62751e. It is joined to a space and the known skill title produced by the
  native skill-name formatter at 0x629b66 / 0x627586. The existing scoped
  Skill rating: Not -> 未掌握 li row supplies its meaning. The general
  Not {s} -> 非{s} template is not the skill rating's authored translation.
  Positive ratings and names come from the established scoped skill glossary;
  their complete productions are generated domains, not this renderer's
  fixed literal sentences.

* Affects skills: and Affected by attributes: head variable native lists.
  Their items come from the native skill-name or attribute-name domains.
  The optional " (minor)" suffix marks the lower contribution branch, while
  Affects no skills. is the complete empty-list sentence. (etc) is a native
  continuation marker when the allotted display cannot show the entire list.
  All these fixed operands already have reviewed Chinese translations.

* Native single spaces, comma-space, and quote operands are tagged
  dynamic-separator. They retain punctuation until the complete string owner
  formats the target. They are not missing words and should not receive
  dictionary replacements merely because they have no standalone target.

Generated common-header values:

The unit display-name formatter call is reference 0x6282dc / classic 0x625cfc.
The quoted unit gloss formatter is 0x628331 / 0x625d51. Literal quotes are
applied at 0x628336 and 0x628381 / 0x625d56 and 0x625da1. These values may
contain procedural language words, given names, surnames, nicknames, authored
names, and native creature/caste qualifiers. They are not a finite list of
sample people to hardcode into this page's dictionary. Retain the established
native_unit_identity formatter/capture ownership and
src/native_unit_identity_translation.inc / src/native_unit_identity_rows.inc
translation pipeline.

The creature/caste formatter is 0x62848c / 0x625eac. The native header then
capitalizes its result and trims the visible creature-name field to at most
24 characters at reference 0x62849d..0x6284be / classic 0x625ebd..0x625ede.
The comma-space operand is at 0x6284e2 / 0x625f02. Native sex branches append
CP437 byte 0x0c for female and 0x0b for male at 0x6284f9 / 0x628502 and
0x625f19 / 0x625f22; other native sex values add neither symbol. Species,
caste and identity are supplied by the existing native-unit/creature grammar;
the sex symbols remain native semantic glyphs. Arbitrary screenshots or
sample names must not be turned into a replacement glossary.

The existing_translation column records authored data and its exact location,
not a claim that game pixels were observed or that every semantic field owner
is correctly applied. Translation availability and native source ownership
are distinct: this screenshot's Above + Average strength split is a matcher
ownership bug even though the full attribute phrases already exist.
